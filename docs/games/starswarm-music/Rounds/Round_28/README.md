# Round 28 — Coordinated-force opening

**Status:** pending owner audition (November 11, 2026).

## Hypothesis
The sound missing from Round 27 is **coordinated force**, not an isolated louder lead or faster individual notes. BG-1's opening has a rapid build into sustained musical mass; Round 27 began loud but lost energy. The previously approved swarm and astronomical spotlight create perceived force through aligned voices, register expansion, shared musical direction and dynamic handoffs.

## Experiment
- **`starswarm-round28-coordinated-force-opening.mp3`** (22.056 s): original from-silence opening, multiple coordinated electromagnetic/inharmonic voices, harmonic development and moving bass. No old ambience/recording is reused, and no copyrighted reference music is sampled.
- **`starswarm_round28_force_opening.py`**: local deterministic Python generator, distributed together with the audio in ChatGPT as `StarSwarm_Round28_Audio_and_Source.zip`.
- This is deliberately **only 22 seconds**, not a full new BGM. The experiment evaluates whether arrival strength continues beyond the opening rather than falling away.

### Preliminary reference observations
Comparing initial excerpts at low sample rate found BG-1's first second substantially quieter than seconds 2–8, during which its level rose and stayed high; Round 27 was loud at the beginning then receded significantly. The approved strong swarm and upward rise provide different examples of a coordinated foreground musical event. These are diagnostic comparisons, not instructions to reproduce BG-1's notes.

### Approval gate
Does the new opening feel **forceful, cinematic, unmistakably science fiction, coherent and still advancing after the initial arrival**? If not, reject without extending it to seven minutes. Judge at matched perceived volume and eventually beneath continuous gameplay SFX.
