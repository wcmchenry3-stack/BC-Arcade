# Round 41 — Full-length Star Swarm BGM storyboard and roadmap

**Status: proposed long-form composition roadmap (November 12, 2026).** Owner approved the Round 40B creative *direction* and specifically requested a full storyboard with **recurring but varied** signature sounds. This document is a composition plan, not a finished score, locked cue sheet, release authorization, or confirmation that the 7-minute music has been rendered.

## North star

**Urgency first; power second; distinctly dark, cinematic science-fiction character throughout.** Serious but not relentlessly depressing, adventurous without being jovial, courageous without being fearless. Musical momentum persists even while the main phrase becomes quieter. The independent BGM does **not** follow enemy waves, the Carrier, firing, game-state transitions, or other live events. Star Swarm's continuous battle/SFX supply immediate tactical excitement; the score supplies atmosphere and conviction.

**Proven building blocks:** Round 34B's directed musical force, 35B's faster phrase pacing, 37B's moving undercurrent during quiet moments, 38's connected developing phrases, and Round 40B's clear astronomical rises, varied robotic signals, Gathering Swarm Chorus, recognizable Electromagnetic Choir and audible descending Shepard with music underneath. Preserve their synthesis techniques and musical vocabulary. They are not permission to paste an unchanged 90-second demo four times.

## Length and musical structure

**Working target ~7:00**, flexibly **6:30–7:30** depending on musical form. One continuous evolving composition. The old 11:45 four-chapter structure and mandatory final duration are obsolete. Aim for a long arc of musical ideas, not a list of equally spaced effects:

| Working span | Musical objective | Development requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:10 | **Determined arrival** | Immediate sense of force and purposeful motion; develop theme A with sharper harmonic direction and bright/dark tension instead of static pads |
| 1:10–2:20 | **Gathering pressure** | Theme A fragments become stronger harmonic counter-movement; introduce distinct theme B with dramatic urgency, not copied from source tracks |
| 2:20–3:30 | **Uncertainty with conviction** | Different chord colors, lower density and a still-moving undercurrent; motif B changes register and phrase length, without losing energy |
| 3:30–4:40 | **Renewed engagement** | Develop theme C/new melodic contour and altered bass movement; build force through directional articulations, not extra noisy layers |
| 4:40–5:50 | **Wider and darker** | Return of earlier themes reharmonized and revoiced; meaningful release and renewed suspense, with breathing room |
| 5:50–7:00 | **Unresolved continuation** | Stronger motion and recognizable but transformed opening idea; prepare seamless musical re-entry to beginning, no triumphant final cadence |

These span titles are **internal composing prompts**, not dramatic game events and not separate audio tracks. Arrange overlaps freely.

## Variations without cloning

- **Astronomical rises:** first expansive, second perhaps more urgent/shorter or reharmonized. Preserve the audible glide and successful rise → music return. Do not transpose/re-pitch an existing licensed source; re-render our own synthetic signature.
- **Robotic signals:** varied rhythms, pitches, order and intensity. Several foreground individual gestures can form a phrase; if introducing a chorus, allow a connection without removing the intervening foundation-only breathing time whenever that is the listening priority.
- **Gathering Swarm Chorus:** signature multi-voice/quivering foreground timbre must remain. Change interval voicing, musical interaction, register or entrance to suit the returning theme. Not identical audio pasted twice.
- **Electromagnetic Choir:** distinct from strong Gathering Swarm, can move harmonically/expand in a later return. Keep the previously approved Round 40 treatment recognizable.
- **Descending Shepard:** audible and intelligible while underlying music still plays; keep up-rises spatially and temporally separate. Alter duration/intensity/pitch bed without losing the perceptual illusion.
- **Distant organ:** optional color within selected phrases, always elusive and sci-fi; avoid earlier Renaissance-fair instrumentation.

**Every variation should have musical motivation** (different harmony, tension stage or phrase destination), not randomization for its own sake. Never add unrelated concurrent notes as a cheap proxy for orchestral scale.

## How to build and review the full track

1. **Storyboard signoff / musical score map.** Agree the emotional arc, signature density and long-form premise; maintain a flexible timeline. This document is the proposal.
2. **Compose new connective material and phrase-level variations.** Build the 6–8-minute uninterrupted **foundation-only** rough first, ensuring *ordinary* stretches are not monotonous or just repeated prior MP3 sections. Evaluate representative excerpt seams, quiet passages and thematic returns. Existing approved procedural generator techniques may be reused, but not licensed original recordings/melodies.
3. **Arrange signature variants over that score.** Keep them distinct; retain Round 40's Shepard audibility and astronomical entrance, with 10–20 s musical breathing intervals as a guide. Check if density works musically rather than rigidly following the map. Render a **full creative rough** and optional short excerpt A/Bs for problematic transitions; no game integration yet.
4. **Owner long-form review.** Check urgency, power, dark sci-fi identity, variation over multiple minutes, relative prominence, no bloated effect soup, natural transitions and whether every theme returns with purpose.
5. **Game mix & delivery validation.** Listen under real sustained combat SFX at actual BGM gain of 0.2 on phone speakers and headphones; control level/loudness without squashing short-term dynamics. Validate mono and stereo. Master with a listening-justified codec/bitrate, aiming below the original **11.28 MB total asset budget**.
6. **Loop & distributed listening.** Create genuinely musical, encoder/device-tested seamless loop. Identify new **phrase-safe start offsets** for first visit vs returning visits; **Round 23 cue times (0,60,138,234,268,358) are obsolete**. On consecutive games within one visit resume exact musical position; across visits advance through approved safe phrase entries to expose varied music, using local state. No gameplay synchronization and no network state.
7. **Only after acceptance:** update the game audio integration and removal of third-party background files, rights logs, source design log, app docs and automated tests under the existing epic, with PR review. **Do not merge experiment archives as production audio.**

## Acceptance criteria for the next full rough

- Music feels urgent, powerful and coherent from the opening and during **10–20 s foundation-only gaps**.
- At least **three musically distinct underlying phrase/harmonic ideas**, with meaningful development and a transformed return—not looping Round 38 or Round 40 under accents.
- Each signature remains distinguishable and generally has breathing room before the next featured identity; no rise-to-Shepard collision.
- Multiple instances of a signature can be **varied and purposefully reharmonized/revoiced**, not cloned audio.
- Swarm and electromagnetic choir are distinct and prominent when intended; Shepard descent is *actually audible* over the score.
- Full piece is not a sequence of timestamped demos; neither abrupt cut-ins nor repetitive FX duty cycle.
- Preserve reproducibility, copyright independence and provenance. Metrics aid verification but do not substitute for owner listening.
