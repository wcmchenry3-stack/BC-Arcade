# Round 30 — Harmonic refinement of Round 29B

**Status:** awaiting owner audition (November 11, 2026). Not a production/game asset.

## Why
Round 29B retained the desired electromagnetic space identity and increasing sense of force, but the owner felt the voices were **clashy and insufficiently musical**. This round is a single controlled harmonic/voicing revision—not a new layer or another seven-minute composition.

## Original 26-second audition

- `starswarm-round30-C-harmonic-electromagnetic.mp3` — **26.064 s**, 24 kHz stereo, 128 kbps, **417,452 bytes**, **−13.4 LUFS**, true peak **−2.1 dBTP**
- `starswarm_round30_harmonic_refinement.py` — from-silence procedural source, derived and revised from the original Round 29 diagnostic generator. Does not read or reuse any previous MP3 or copyrighted recording.
- Comparison/control: `Rounds/Round_29/starswarm-round29-B-electromagnetic-lead.mp3` (**22.056 s**, **−13.6 LUFS**).

## What changed
- Preserved the original coordinated ensemble buildup, sub-bass, energy envelope, spatial reflections and electronically modulated melody.
- Revoiced the harmonic arc to **D minor → B-flat major 7 → G minor 7 → A suspended dominant → D minor** with intentional voice leading and more common tones.
- Reduced some upper inharmonic partial strength in field/coil tones, and narrowed the signal lead's FM depth; retained its electronic, non-orchestral identity.
- Adjusted a small number of lead notes and shortened overlapping melodic releases where they produced unresolved collisions.
- Added ~4 seconds for a short, dark but audible harmonic arrival rather than a disconnected cutoff.
- Maintained single coherent scoring from silence, *not* Round 23/29 music plus an overlay.

## Decision gate

**Is it more musical and less unpleasantly clashy, without weakening the powerful electromagnetic buildup?** Judge especially the last 10 seconds. If it sounds more conventional/fantasy or loses energy, don't extend it yet.
