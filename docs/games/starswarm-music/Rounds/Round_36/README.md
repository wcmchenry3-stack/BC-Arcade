## Composition

- **First idea (~0:00–0:22)** retains the approved **Round 35B 0.87 timing factor** and Round 34B's controlled low/mid balance and musical energy contour. The melodic line retains the original expressive electronic character.
- **Second idea (~0:22–0:47.5)** shifts to a new melodic contour (a descent and then more urgent upward pressure), new harmonic sequence **Gm9 → E♭maj7 → B♭ → A-suspended → Dm**, changing accents and cadential purpose. It overlaps the previous idea through shared tones instead of stopping abruptly.
- No old MP3 recordings, sample overlays, licensed material, extra choir, atmospheric rise, Shepard fall or robotic-signal effects appear. The score is synthesized from original note/voice source only.
- Note: the track's brief ending fade exists **only for audition delivery**, not as a loop or final soundtrack arrangement. This experiment does not establish a full seven-minute form or verified gapless loop.

## What the owner should judge

1. Does the second phrase **feel like a meaningful continuation**, or a separate section/copy-paste?
2. Does it retain **urgency, power, seriousness and sci-fi character** throughout, not just in the opening?
3. Is the second phrase actually **musically distinctive** enough to help eventually sustain a longer score?
4. Does the transition at roughly **0:21–0:25** feel natural, with no loss of momentum?
