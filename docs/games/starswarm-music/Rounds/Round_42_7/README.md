## Owner feedback — Round 42.7 is a giant step forward; shift hierarchy toward ambient bed (November 12, 2026)

The owner describes Round 42.7 as a **giant step in the right direction**. Its palette and atmosphere therefore deserve preservation, not replacement. The remaining mismatch versus the original BG-1/BG-2/BG-3 is mainly one of **foreground/background hierarchy and emotional weight**:

- Original beds: broad electronic drones, amorphous evolving pads, subtle sci-fi/noise movement; neutral/suspenseful and able to sit beneath gameplay.
- Round 42.7: despite improvement, its soaring organ-like choral/possibly acoustic-sounding voice carries **too much explicit melody and ceremonial/solemn emotional gravity**.
- The result feels like a standalone cinematic focal movement requiring attention, not a low-profile continuous atmospheric bed.

**Round 42.8 proposed controlled refinement:** preserve Round 42.7's open-ended electronic harmony, internal motion and warm sustained melodic vocabulary, but **reverse the apparent listening hierarchy**. Make the drone/pad texture rich and independently interesting, introduce subtle electronic/noise spectral evolution and depth, reduce prominence and frequency of the organ-like lead (not eliminate it), let melodic phrases emerge briefly and recede, and soften obvious harmonic/cadential arrivals. Aim for *environmental, suspenseful cosmic current* rather than a solemn hymn or triumphant/ceremonial emotional lift. Foreground/background ratios are subjective, not mandated literal gain values. Don't solve by merely turning the master down, adding lots of busy voices, or abandoning the seven-minute structural development requirement.

**Test scope:** short foundation-only A/B against Round 42.7; no astronomical rise, robotic signals, swarm/EM choir or Shepard yet. Judge primarily whether B functions comfortably as background under shooter activity while retaining musical life, mystery and warmth. Do not treat Round 42.7 as final-approved production music.
