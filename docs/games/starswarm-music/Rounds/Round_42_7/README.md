# Round 42.7 — Cosmic Motion (foundation-only A/B)

**Status: owner listening pending, November 12, 2026.** Round 42.6 was closer, but the owner found it too **acoustic/organ-string-polyphonic, liturgical, melancholically cadential and march-like** versus original BG-1/2/3's deep continuous electronic drones, electronic pads, shimmering spatial trails and open-ended sci-fi suspense. This experiment revises the *palette* and harmonic movement, not the seven-minute form.

## Audio
- **A** — `starswarm-round42_7-A-round42_6-control.mp3`: exact previous 42.6 cosmic hymn control.
- **B** — `starswarm-round42_7-B-cosmic-motion.mp3`: **new original, foundation-only 60-second synth score**: E drone/open fifth continuity and gradually moving suspended/modal pitch clusters with soft transitions; one sparse, expressive, gently vibrato-inflected legato *electronic* lead; animated, stereo detuned cosmic pads with oscillator motion; wide, filtered reverberant halo. No busy polyphonic organ/string section, no cadential major/minor march progression and no rhythmic accented ostinato.
- **Neither track** has an astronomical rise, Shepard effect, Gathering Swarm Chorus, Electromagnetic Choir, robotic signals or other previously approved foreground sci-fi signatures.
- Both 60.048 seconds, 24 kHz stereo, 128 kbps MP3 and approximately **−23.0 LUFS** integrated. These are **not** mastered, gameplay-mixed, or loop-ready.
- The original Python source is `starswarm_round42_7_cosmic_motion.py`, deterministic using numpy/scipy/soundfile/ffmpeg. B independently synthesizes its music and does not sample or layer A, older BGs, or any other existing audio.

## Owner listening gate

1. Is **B more distinctly electronic and cosmic** versus the bowed-string/church-organ acoustic density in A?
2. Do the harmonic motion and the lead feel **open-ended / suspended and expressive**, rather than mournful or liturgical?
3. Does its slow internal oscillating movement create **ongoing cosmic current**, rather than march-like gravity, while keeping a sense of purpose?
4. Is the lead appropriately soaring and audible, or has the sparse arrangement gone too far?
5. **Only if approved**, use this vocabulary to inform a new full-length foundation retaining Round 42's proven non-repetitive structure.
