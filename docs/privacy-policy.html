Privacy Policy

BC Arcade — Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

BC Arcade is a free collection of classic games for iOS and Android, also playable at games.buffingchi.com. It is published by Buffing Chi ("we", "us"). There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is kept, and how to delete it.

What We Collect

An anonymous session ID

On first launch the app generates a random identifier and stores it on your device. It is sent to our server with each request so that your scores and statistics can be kept together. It is not derived from your device, your name or any other identifier, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Because it can single out one installation of the app, privacy laws such as the GDPR treat it as pseudonymous personal data, and we handle it that way.

Gameplay data

Linked to that session ID, our server stores:

  • Which game you played, when it started and finished, and how long it lasted
  • The outcome and final score
  • Game settings for that session (for example difficulty or variant)
  • A log of in-game events such as moves, used to show statistics and diagnose bugs
  • Which games your session is entitled to open

Statistics, leaderboard rankings, Arcade XP, player level and daily challenge progress are calculated from these records. When you are offline, results are queued on your device and sent the next time the app has a connection.

Automatic error logs

When the app hits a warning or error, it may send our server a short log entry — severity, the part of the app involved, the error message and technical context — linked to your session ID. We use these only to find and fix bugs.

Crash and performance reports (Sentry)

The app and our server use Sentry (sentry.io) for crash reporting and performance monitoring. A report can include the error message and stack trace, app version, device model, operating system version, device state such as free memory and storage, and timing measurements. We have Sentry's option to send personal identifiers turned off, and reports never contain a name or email because we do not have one. Your anonymous session ID is removed from server reports before they are sent. Sentry keeps this data for 90 days.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app feedback form, we receive what you submit: the title and description you type, a screenshot if you attach one, and the app's recent warning and error messages to help us reproduce the problem. Server logs

Like any internet service, our server records each request: IP address, time, the path requested, status code and response time. We use this for security, rate limiting and troubleshooting. These logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period and are not combined with your gameplay data.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts — there are no accounts
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No location data
  • No payment information — version 1.0 has no purchases
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your anonymous session ID
  • Preferences such as theme, language and sound
  • Games in progress, so they survive closing the app
  • Results and event logs waiting to be sent while you are offline
  • A cached copy of which games your session may open

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Why We Use This Data (Legal Bases)

  • To provide the games you asked for — saving results, statistics, progress and leaderboards (performance of our agreement with you under the Terms of Service).
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and reliable — crash reports, error logs, server logs, rate limiting and preventing abuse of leaderboards.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Who Processes It and Where

We use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf. All of them store it in the United States:

  • Render — hosts our application server (Oregon, USA)
  • Supabase (on Amazon Web Services) — hosts our database (Oregon, USA)
  • Sentry — crash and performance reports
  • Cloudflare — DNS and network routing for our domains

If you are outside the United States, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States. Where the law requires it, those transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing terms.

How Long We Keep It

  • Gameplay data and error logs: until you delete them (see below). [OWNER TO CONFIRM: a maximum retention period for inactive sessions.]
  • Sentry reports: 90 days

  • Data on your device: until you delete your data in the app or uninstall it

Deleting Your Data

Open Settings in the app, choose Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes the game records, event logs, entitlements and error logs linked to your session ID from our server, and clears the session ID and any queued results from your device. It cannot be undone. The next time you play, the app starts a new anonymous session.

Because we do not know who you are, the in-app button is the only way we can reliably find your records. If you cannot use it, email buffingchi@gmail.com and we will help.
