Privacy Policy

BC Arcade — Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

BC Arcade is a free collection of classic games for iOS and Android, also playable at games.buffingchi.com. It is published by Buffing Chi ("we", "us"). There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The leaderboards are optional and public, and show a name we generate for you, never text you type. This policy explains what the app collects, why, what is public, where it is kept, and how to remove or delete it.

What We Collect

An anonymous session ID

On first launch the app generates a random identifier and stores it on your device. It is sent to our server with each request so that your scores and statistics can be kept together. It is not derived from your device, your name or any other identifier, and we never ask you for your name, email address or phone number. Because it can single out one installation of the app, privacy laws such as the GDPR treat it as pseudonymous personal data, and we handle it that way.

Gameplay data

Linked to that session ID, our server stores:

  • Which game you played, when it started and finished, and how long it lasted
  • The outcome and final score
  • Game settings for that session (for example difficulty or variant)
  • A log of in-game events such as moves, used to show statistics and diagnose bugs
  • Which games your session is entitled to open
  • Your guesses in the Daily Word puzzle, kept for that day's puzzle and removed automatically after 14 days

Statistics, leaderboard rankings, Arcade XP, player level and daily challenge progress are calculated from these records. When you are offline, results are queued on your device and sent the next time the app has a connection.

Public leaderboards (optional)

You are on no leaderboard unless you choose to join. If you press Join leaderboards (in Profile, or from the prompt after a result), our server gives you a generated name such as "Brave Otter 4821". You cannot type your own name, and names are not unique.

  • What other players can see: your generated name, your score on that board, your rank and the date. Nothing you typed, and not your session ID.
  • What we keep: the generated name is stored with your anonymous session ID, and every finished game from this installation is ranked under it. Changing your name (Get a new name) changes it on all of those scores.
  • How to leave: Profile → Leave leaderboards deletes your generated name and removes you from every leaderboard at once. Joining again gives a new name. Your private game saves and statistics are stored only on your device and are never uploaded; Delete my data does not remove them, but uninstalling the app (or clearing its data on Android) does (see below).
  • How to erase everything: Settings → Delete my data also deletes your generated name.

Earlier versions of the app let you type a name. If you typed one then, it is not shown publicly: you were moved to a generated name, but a copy of what you typed may still sit in the private record of those games on our server (visible only to your session). Delete my data removes it.

Automatic error logs

When the app hits a warning or error, it may send our server a short log entry — severity, the part of the app involved, the error message and technical context — linked to your session ID. We use these only to find and fix bugs.

Crash and performance reports (Sentry) — diagnostics

Unlike the data above, these reports are not needed to play the games; they help us find and fix problems, and they are sent automatically. The app and our server use Sentry (sentry.io) for crash reporting and performance monitoring. A report can include the error message and stack trace, app version, device model, operating system version, device state such as free memory and storage, and timing measurements. Our server also sends Sentry a sample of server-side performance traces (how long requests take); these carry no installation identifier and our request headers are scrubbed from them. We have Sentry's option to send personal identifiers turned off, and reports never contain a name or email because we do not have one. Your anonymous session ID is removed from server reports before they are sent. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses [OWNER TO CONFIRM: Sentry "Prevent Storing of IP Addresses" enabled]. Sentry's software does give each installation its own random identifier, unrelated to our session ID, so reports from one installation can be recognised as coming from the same app install. Reports may also include a short trail of recent app events (for example which screen you opened, or which of our server addresses the app called). Sentry keeps this data for 90 days [OWNER TO CONFIRM: Sentry retention setting]. There is currently no in-app switch for these reports; to object, email us (see Contact). Because we cannot tell which Sentry reports are yours, Delete my data does not reach them; they expire on their own.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app feedback form, what you submit is sent to Sentry, the same service we use for crash reports: the title and description you type, whether it is a bug report or a suggestion, and, if there are any, a text file of the app's recent warning and error messages to help us reproduce the problem. It is not published anywhere, it is not linked to a name or email because we do not ask for one, it is tied only to the random Sentry installation identifier described above, and Sentry keeps it for 90 days [OWNER TO CONFIRM: Sentry retention setting]. Please do not include personal information in feedback.

Server logs

Like any internet service, our server records each request: IP address, time, the path requested, status code and response time. We use this for security, rate limiting and troubleshooting. These logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period and are not combined with your gameplay data.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No real name, email address, phone number or contacts — there are no accounts. The only name we collect or publish is the optional generated leaderboard name. Any labels you type yourself (for example seat names in Hearts) stay on your device and are never uploaded
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No location data
  • No payment information — version 1.0 has no purchases
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your anonymous session ID
  • Your generated leaderboard name, if you joined
  • Preferences such as theme, language and sound
  • Games in progress and statistics, so they survive closing the app
  • Labels you type yourself, such as Hearts player names
  • Results and event logs waiting to be sent while you are offline
  • A cached copy of which games your session may open

Delete my data removes your data from our server and clears your session ID, generated name and queued results on the device. Local game saves, statistics, preferences and typed labels are never uploaded and are removed by uninstalling the app (or clearing its data on Android).

Why We Use This Data (Legal Bases)

  • To provide the games you asked for — saving results, statistics, progress and leaderboards (performance of our agreement with you under the Terms of Service).
  • Your choice — showing you on the public leaderboards, which only happens after you press Join and stops when you leave.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and reliable — crash reports, error logs, server logs, rate limiting and preventing abuse of leaderboards.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Who Processes It and Where

We use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf. All of them store it in the United States:

  • Render — hosts our application server (Oregon, USA)
  • Supabase (on Amazon Web Services) — hosts our database (Oregon, USA)
  • Sentry — crash and performance reports
  • Cloudflare — DNS and network routing for our domains; our server traffic passes through it. It is not used for feedback or analytics

If you are outside the United States, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States. Where the law requires it, those transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing terms.

  • Data on your device: session ID, generated name and queued results until you use Delete my data; everything else until you uninstall the app (or clear its data on Android)

Leaving the Leaderboards and Deleting Your Data

To leave the leaderboards only, open Profile and choose Leave leaderboards. To delete everything, open Settings in the app, choose Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes the game records, event logs, Daily Word guesses, entitlements, error logs and generated leaderboard name linked to your session ID from our server, and clears the session ID and any queued results from your device. It cannot be undone. The next time you play, the app starts a new anonymous session.

Because we do not know who you are, the in-app button is the only way we can reliably find your records. If you cannot use it, email buffingchi@gmail.com and we will help.
