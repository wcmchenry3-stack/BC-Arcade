Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. BC Arcade has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. The app creates a random anonymous ID on your device so your scores and statistics stay together.

Does BC Arcade cost anything?

No. The current version is free and has no in-app purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, choose Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes the game records, statistics, Daily Word guesses, error logs and leaderboard name linked to your anonymous ID from our server, and clears that ID from your device. It cannot be undone. The next time you play, the app starts a new anonymous session.

Game saves, preferences and names you type yourself (for example player names in a card game) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. To remove them, uninstall the app (or, on Android, clear the app's storage).

If you cannot use the button, email us and we will help. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask you to send the request from the app.

How do leaderboards work, and how do I leave them?

Leaderboards are optional. You appear on them only after you choose Join leaderboards in Profile or after a finished game. We give you a generated name, such as "Brave Otter 4821"; you cannot type your own. Get a new name changes it on all your scores.

To leave, open Profile and choose Leave leaderboards. Your generated name is deleted and you are removed from every leaderboard at once. Joining again gives you a new name.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Games work without a connection. Results are saved on your device and sent the next time the app is online; leaderboards and the daily puzzles need a connection to update.

I lost my scores after reinstalling. Can you restore them?

Usually not. Scores are linked to the anonymous ID stored in the app's data on your device. Restoring your phone from a device backup may bring that ID back, but because there are no accounts we cannot find or move your scores to a new installation or device for you.
