Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. BC Arcade has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. The app creates a random anonymous ID on your device so your scores and statistics stay together.

Does BC Arcade cost anything?

No. The current version is free and has no in-app purchases.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data below.

How do leaderboards work, and how do I leave them?

Leaderboards are optional. You appear on them only after you choose Join leaderboards in Profile or after a finished game. We give you a generated name, such as "Brave Otter 4821"; you cannot type your own. Get a new name changes it on all your scores.

To leave, open Profile and choose Leave leaderboards. Your generated name is deleted and you are removed from every leaderboard at once. Joining again gives you a new name.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Games work without a connection. Results are saved on your device and sent the next time the app is online; leaderboards and the daily puzzles need a connection to update.

I lost my scores after reinstalling. Can you restore them?

Usually not. Scores are linked to the anonymous ID stored in the app's data on your device. Restoring your phone from a device backup may bring that ID back, but because there are no accounts we cannot find or move your scores to a new installation or device for you.

Delete your data (BC Arcade by Buffing Chi)

BC Arcade has no sign-in. Instead, the app creates a random anonymous ID on your device the first time you open it, and your game records on our server are linked to that ID. Deleting your data deletes everything linked to that ID. You do not need to reinstall the app to do it.

Step 1: Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open BC Arcade and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm with Delete my data.

This is immediate and cannot be undone. The next time you play, the app starts a new anonymous ID with no history. If you only want to disappear from the public leaderboards, use Profile → Leave leaderboards instead.

Step 2: Remove what is stored only on your device

Game saves, games in progress, on-device statistics, preferences and names you type yourself (for example player names in a card game) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Uninstall the app to remove them (on Android you can also clear the app's storage in system settings).

What is deleted

From our server, permanently and straight away:

  • Your game records: which games you played, when, scores, outcomes, settings and in-game event logs (your statistics, leaderboard rankings, Arcade XP and level are calculated from these)
  • Your Daily Word guesses
  • Which games your ID is entitled to open
  • Automatic error logs linked to your ID
  • Your generated leaderboard name, which removes you from every leaderboard

From your device: your anonymous ID, your generated leaderboard name and any results still waiting to be sent.
