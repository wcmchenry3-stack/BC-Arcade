# Star Swarm Sprite Credits

The sprites in this folder began as Kenney.nl packs released under CC0 1.0 Universal (Public
Domain); their sources were recorded in PR #863 when they were added, and this file keeps that
record in the repo (#2492). Since #3595 every sprite in this folder is original; the replaced
sprites' Kenney sources are recorded at the end of this file.

The player ship layers, Buddy, the enemies (the `enemy/` folder), the asteroids (the `rock/`
folder), the projectiles (the `shot/` folder) and the pickups (the `pickup/` folder) are original
art, generated in-house by `tools/assets/draw_starswarm_art.py` (epic #3590, #3592, #3600, #3593,
#3596, #3594, #3595; design log `docs/games/starswarm-art-design.md`). They use no third-party
art. As sprites were replaced by generated ones, their Kenney rows were removed (the power-ups
were the last, #3595, and their separate `powerups/CREDITS.md` went with them).
