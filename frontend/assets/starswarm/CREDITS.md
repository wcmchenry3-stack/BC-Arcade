# Star Swarm Sprite Credits

Most sprites in this folder derive from Kenney.nl packs released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(Public Domain). No attribution required, but appreciated. Sources were recorded in PR #863
when the sprites were added; this file keeps that record in the repo (#2492).

The player ship layers, Buddy and the enemies (the `enemy/` folder) are original art, generated
in-house by `tools/assets/draw_starswarm_art.py` (epic #3590, #3592, #3600, #3593; design log
`docs/games/starswarm-art-design.md`). They use no third-party art. As more sprites are replaced
by generated ones, their Kenney rows are removed.

Power-up sprites are credited separately in [`powerups/CREDITS.md`](powerups/CREDITS.md).
