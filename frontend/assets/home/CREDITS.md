# Home Screen Icon Credits

Images on the home screen's game tiles. Every one is original art generated in-house. The only
third-party elements are the numeral and letter outlines on the 2048, Sudoku, Daily Word, Blackjack
and Hearts tiles (Space Grotesk Bold, under the SIL OFL 1.1, `tools/assets/lobby_numerals.OFL.txt`)
and the red dragon 中 on the Mahjong tile (Yuji Syuku, under the SIL OFL 1.1,
`tools/assets/mahjong_glyphs.OFL.txt`, as on the game's own tiles). The Blackjack, Hearts,
Mahjong, Star Swarm and Cascade tiles are for hidden games: a store build never packages them
(#4013). `src/__tests__/assetCredits.test.ts` fails if a file here has no row, and
`src/__tests__/lobbyTileBudget.test.ts` caps each tile at 3,400 B.
